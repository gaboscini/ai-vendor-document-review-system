# Data Processing Addendum

## Subprocessors

The current subprocessor list is maintained on the provider trust portal. Material changes are communicated through the portal subscription mechanism.

## Deletion and return

Customer production data is scheduled for deletion after contract termination. Backups may be retained for up to 90 days following termination of services before routine expiration.

## International transfers

Where applicable, transfers use the contractual mechanism identified in the applicable order form and supporting transfer documentation.
