# Incident Response Policy

## Detection and triage

Security events are triaged by the incident-response team according to severity, affected systems, and potential customer impact.

## Customer notification

Affected customers will be notified without undue delay after confirmation of a security incident. Notifications include the known scope, mitigation actions, and a point of contact when available.

## Post-incident review

Material incidents receive a documented post-incident review with corrective actions assigned to accountable owners.
