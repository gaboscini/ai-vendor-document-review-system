# Demonstration guide

## Scenario

A security reviewer receives a four-document vendor package. The internal standard requires incident notification within 24 hours and deletion of terminated-customer backups within 30 days.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the assessment workspace.
2. Explain the risk score, control coverage, and evaluation indicators.
3. Select **Incident-notification deadline is not defined**.
4. Show the exact quote and page reference.
5. Ask the assistant: `What is the incident notification deadline?`
6. Confirm that it does not invent a deadline.
7. Accept or reject the finding to demonstrate human decision ownership.
8. Review the backup-retention mismatch and supported encryption control.
9. Explain how reviewer outcomes become regression-test signals.

## What this demonstrates

- evidence-first AI decisions;
- hybrid retrieval and citations;
- structured risk findings;
- human approval;
- evaluation-driven development;
- production-oriented API and data architecture.
